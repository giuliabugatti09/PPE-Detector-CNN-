
Hard Hat Workers - v2 raw
==============================

This dataset was exported via roboflow.ai on May 26, 2020 at 7:06 AM GMT

It includes 7041 images.
Workers are annotated in Multi-Class Classification format.

The following pre-processing was applied to each image:
* Auto-orientation of pixel data (with EXIF-orientation stripping)

No image augmentation techniques were applied.
